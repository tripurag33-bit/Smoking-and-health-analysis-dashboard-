#### Project Overview:Smoking and Health Analysis Dashboard
**Project Description**
The **Smoking and Health Analysis Dashboard** is an exploratory healthcare analytics project developed for **Sapphire IQ** to examine patient smoking behaviors, organ-level health conditions, demographic trends, and clinical biomarkers across a cohort of **10,000 patients**. Designed for management-level reporting and interactive data exploration in **Microsoft Power BI**, the solution transforms raw patient-level records into dynamic KPIs, DAX-calculated risk segments, and multi-dimensional visual analytics without making diagnostic or causal medical claims.
### Core Analytical Objectives & Deliverables
 1. **Executive Health KPIs:** Tracks overall cohort metrics, including **Total Patients (10,000)**, **Current Smokers (3,015 — 30.15%)**, **Patients with a Recorded Organ Category (7,227 — 72.27%)**, and **Patients with a Recorded Health Condition (5,772 — 57.72%)**.
 2. **Feature Engineering & DAX Segmentation:** Enriches patient records with calculated columns for **Age Group** (18–29, 30–44, 45–59, 60+), **Blood Pressure Risk Category** (Lower Range, Elevated, High based on systolic and diastolic readings), and **Estimated Smoking Exposure** in pack-years (None, Low <5, Moderate 5–15, High >15).
 3. **Clinical & Smoking Pattern Analysis:** Evaluates daily cigarette consumption across smoking statuses (averaging **17.1 cigarettes/day** for current smokers), maps 15 specific diagnoses across major organs (**Lungs**, **Heart**, **Liver**, **Kidneys**, and systemic conditions), and compares total cholesterol levels across blood pressure risk tiers.
 4. **Demographic & Geographic Performance:** Analyzes smoking prevalence across age brackets (peaking at **31.4%** in the **45–59** group), condition rates across cumulative pack-year exposure, and regional disparities across five geographic zones (**North**, **West**, **South**, **East**, and **Central**).
